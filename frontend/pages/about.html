WHO WE ARE

Who we are

Project X Contractors was built on a simple idea: homeowners deserve a contractor who answers the phone, explains the price line by line, and finishes on the date they were promised. From our office in McLean, Virginia, we work with homeowners across the Washington, D.C. area.

Our work ranges from kitchen remodels and interior rebuilds to decks, porches and backyard hardscapes, along with restoration after water damage. Whether it is a full kitchen or a single room after a leak, we treat every home the same way: protect it, keep the site clean, and get the details right.

Every project follows the same five steps: request, quote, deposit, build and delivery. You see an itemized quote before any work begins, materials are checked before the crew arrives, and we walk through the finished job with you before we call it done.
