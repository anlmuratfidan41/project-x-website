Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Project X Contractors LLC ("Project X", "we") runs this website. This policy explains what information we collect when you use it, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Project requests: your name, email, phone number (optional), project address, project type, preferred meeting time and the details you write about the work.
  • Job applications: your name, email, phone number (optional), the position, what you tell us about your experience and, if you attach one, your résumé.
  • Quotes and payments: the quotes we prepare for you and a record of payments you make to us.
  • Technical data: basic server logs (such as IP address and browser type) used to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, confirm your email address, schedule site meetings and prepare quotes.
  • To manage your project, including scheduling, invoicing and keeping records required for our business.
  • To review job applications and contact candidates.
  • To protect the website against abuse and fraud.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Text messages (SMS)

If you tick the SMS box on a form, we may text you about that request: that we received it, changes to your site meeting, and when your quote is ready. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. SMS consent is not shared with third parties for their marketing.

Services we use

We use trusted providers to run this service: an email delivery provider (for confirmations and quotes), an SMS provider (only if you opt in), and our website hosting provider. They process information only to provide these services to us.

How long we keep information

We keep project and payment records for as long as needed for the project and for our legal, tax and warranty obligations. Job applications are kept for up to 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by calling us at +1 (202) 460-3027 or writing to our office. Some records may need to be kept where the law requires it.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted staff access and secure storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.
